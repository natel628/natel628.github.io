Spectrum TV Packages in Rhode Island

Three main tiers — Spectrum’s current lineup uses the names TV Select Signature, TV Select Plus, and TV Platinum. Older materials may call these Select, Silver, and Gold, but the channel counts and pricing below reflect the current Rhode Island offerings.
Entry Level

TV Select Signature

$100/mo
Standard rate after 1 year: ~$130/mo
150+ channels local + basic cable
  • All local broadcast stations (ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX, PBS, CW)
  • Popular cable nets: ESPN, CNN, HGTV, AMC, FX, Disney Channel
  • Streaming apps included: Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Unlimited, Max (ad-supported), Peacock Premium
  • Free Spectrum TV App for streaming on devices
  • No regional sports networks (RSNs)
Best for households that want the essentials + major streaming apps without paying for sports RSNs.
Most Popular

TV Select Plus

$110/mo
Standard rate after 1 year: ~$140/mo
160+ channels adds regional sports
  • Everything in TV Select Signature
  • Regional Sports Networks (RSNs) — NESN, MSG, SportsNet NY, etc.
  • Extra sports: NFL Network, MLB Network, NBA TV, ACC Network, SEC Network
  • Same streaming apps as Select Signature
  • Ideal for local sports fans (Red Sox, Celtics, Bruins, etc.)
The key upgrade here is live regional sports. If you follow local teams, this is the minimum tier you need.
Premium

TV Platinum

$145/mo
Standard rate after 1 year: ~$175/mo
230+ channels premiums + sports view
  • Everything in TV Select Plus
  • Premium movie channels: HBO, Showtime, STARZ, The Movie Channel, EPIX
  • Sports View add-on (extra sports channels)
  • Entertainment+ tier (additional lifestyle & entertainment nets)
  • Largest included streaming app bundle (HBO Max Standard, etc.)
For households that want everything: movies, premium series, and the widest sports coverage.

What actually changes between the three?

1. Channel count

150+ → 160+ → 230+. The jump from Select Plus to Platinum is the biggest because it adds premium movie networks and the Sports View / Entertainment+ tiers.

2. Regional sports

Only Select Plus and Platinum include RSNs. Select Signature does not carry NESN, MSG, or similar local sports networks.

3. Premium movies

HBO, Showtime, STARZ, and TMC are only in Platinum. Select Signature and Select Plus do not include premium movie channels.

Pricing notes (Rhode Island):

  • Promotional prices shown are for the first 12 months; standard rates apply after that.
  • A Broadcast TV Surcharge (currently around $25/mo) and regional sports fee may apply on top of the package price. Confirm your exact total with Spectrum.
  • Prices and channel availability can vary slightly by zip code within Rhode Island. The tiers above reflect the standard statewide lineup.
  • Traditional “Select / Silver / Gold” naming: Spectrum has largely moved to the “TV Select Signature / Plus / Platinum” branding. If you see “Silver” or “Gold” in older materials, Silver roughly corresponds to a mid-tier with premiums (HBO/Showtime), and Gold adds STARZ/TMC — which today maps most closely to TV Platinum.
