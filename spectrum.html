Spectrum TV Packages — Rhode Island

Three packages. The main differences are local sports and premium movie channels. Popular channels and streaming apps are already included in the base package.

Quick decision guide

  • Just want popular channels + streaming apps? → Signature ($100/mo)
  • Want local teams (Red Sox, Celtics, Bruins)? → Pro ($110/mo)
  • Want local sports + HBO, Showtime & STARZ? → VIP ($145/mo)

The only three things that really change

1. Local Sports

Signature has none.
Pro & VIP include NESN and other regional networks so you can watch local teams.

2. Premium Movies

Signature & Pro have none.
Only VIP includes HBO, Showtime, STARZ and the other movie channels.

3. Extra Sports Pack

Only VIP includes Sports View (NFL RedZone, Golf Channel, etc.) and the larger Entertainment Pack.
