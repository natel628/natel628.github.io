The year at a glance

Each bar is a shower's active range with a tick at its peak — gold rows are running right now, and the bright line is today.

How the numbers work. ZHR is the ideal rate at peak with the radiant directly overhead under a perfectly dark sky — real counts are lower. The radiant is the patch of sky the meteors appear to stream from; the higher it sits, the more of them clear the horizon. A shower's best window is the stretch where the Sun is at least 12° below the horizon and the radiant is above 15°, and every estimate here also accounts for moonlight, your sky setting and any forecast cloud.

Peak dates drift a day or two year to year, radiant positions are approximate, and moon illumination comes from the synodic month, so treat this as planning guidance rather than an ephemeris. Cloud cover comes from the free Open-Meteo forecast, requested straight from your browser.
