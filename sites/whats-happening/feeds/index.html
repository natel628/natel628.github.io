How to subscribe

  1. Apple Calendar: tap a Subscribe link, or choose File, New Calendar Subscription and paste the URL.
  2. Google Calendar: copy a URL above, then in Google Calendar go to Settings, Add calendar, From URL, and paste it.

Feeds appear after the first daily sync and stay valid-but-empty until real listings publish. Your calendar app refreshes subscriptions on its own, usually within a day.
