      About these positions

      The coastline, roads and town footprints are my own schematic drawn against the official poster's A–M / 1–9 grid — not a copy of Rockstar's artwork, and not the game's map tiles.

      Every pin sits inside the grid square its guide puts it in, then is nudged within that square by the direction given ("SE of Hamlet", "northern waterfront"). That is square-level accuracy, not street-level. Where a spot only has a parent label, the square is the label's square.

      Each entry links the guide it came from. Disputed details — code digits, payout ranges, one source per camp — are called out instead of smoothed over.
