    📝 Notes

    Type it, hit + Add note, done — campsites to book, contacts, gear to pack, detours to try.

    📸 Photos, videos & Drive

    Pick one file or a whole batch, add a caption, and enter your name (kyle, chuck, justin, nate) — that's the gate that keeps randos from uploading shit. Tick auto-send and new clips file themselves to the shared Drive folder; or tap ☁️ on anything later. The folder link sits right under the auto-send box.

    🗺️ Stops, food, golf, camping

    Set your start and end on the route map, then add stops in between. Search Golf, Food, or Camping and hit add on anything you like — it lands straight on the route. Every stop has ↑ ↓ buttons to rearrange, and the days, drive times, and gas math reshape themselves.

    ⚙️ Settings

    Top bar or sidebar, hide sections you don't care about, bigger text, 24-hour time, remember-your-name, low-data mode for dead zones — plus backups and the Drive owner setup under Backup & owner.

    💵 Budget

    Add each cost as its own line — food, gas, a campsite, anything that comes up. The total splits four ways, so every line shows what it costs each of you.
