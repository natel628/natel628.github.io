🚐 Road Trip Planner

Sample setup — search vans & RVs, watch prices, and plot your route

1. Find Vans / RVs

Sample listings (no live data yet). Search, filter, and add any rig to your price watch.

2. 💰 Price Watch

Set a target price. Hit “Check prices” to simulate a market update — items at or below target light up green.

3. 🗺️ Trip Map

Add stops by name (e.g. “Grand Canyon”) or click anywhere on the map. Drag to reorder visually — route connects in list order.
