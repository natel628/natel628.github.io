On the houses. The four dynasties you asked for sit either side of two others. Between the Stuarts and the Windsors came the Hanoverians (1714–1901) and the short House of Saxe-Coburg-Gotha, renamed Windsor by George V in 1917 amid anti-German feeling in the First World War. The Hanoverians are shown here in muted steel so the line stays unbroken. And yes — the "Stewards" are properly the Stuarts, from the French spelling of the Scottish surname Stewart; Mary, Queen of Scots brought the name south in 1603.
